01. Journey & Resilience

“Every hardship in the past taught me how to stand tall, work honestly, and never lose my warmth.”

Life was never handed to me easily. Growing up in Pachathar, Nepal, I faced many difficult chapters and deep personal struggles—yet each challenge only strengthened my resolve. Moving far from home to work in Oman, I turned resilience into daily dedication in the kitchen, earning my way with patience, pride, and quiet courage.

02. Personal Story

About Sunita Darnal

Originally from Pachathar, Nepal, Sunita Darnal currently lives and works in Oman as a dedicated cook—carrying a story shaped by perseverance, grace, and hard work.

After enduring many struggles in her earlier years, she stepped forward from Nepal to Oman to build a better future through honest labor. Today, cooking is not only her livelihood—it is a craft she pours care and Nepali hospitality into every single day.

Fluent in Nepali (Native), English (Fluent), and Hindi (Fluent), Sunita balances long hours in the kitchen with her lifelong love for dance, finding joy, rhythm, and strength in every step forward.
